How to play

Current ruleset (v283). Closed playtest — this page is information only.

Goal

Enter the house. Find three evidences. Identify the ghost in the journal. A wrong name is not free.

The lobby

  • Ten minutes on the clock, then the house drops.
  • Someone may talk in chat who is not in the world with you.
  • You may see a figure far off. It is not the hunt yet.

Tools

  • EMF, spirit box, UV, thermometer, writing book, journal.
  • You receive the kit when you first spawn and when you die.
  • The house does not restock the hotbar when the contract starts. Bring what you have.

The house

  • You cannot break the house.
  • Doors and tools still work.
  • Rooms include foyer, living, kitchen, bedroom, study, attic, cellar, and a secret room.
  • Lights go dark when it hunts. They return when it stops.

Evidence

Each night is one of four. You need three signs that match that type.

  • Spirit — EMF, spirit box, writing
  • Shade — EMF, writing, freezing
  • Revenant — EMF, UV, freezing
  • Demon — spirit box, UV, freezing

Identify

  • Open the journal. Tap a name.
  • You cannot guess for one minute after the contract starts.
  • Wrong name: it hunts, sanity drops, and you wait another minute to guess again.
  • Right name: you leave. You keep what you were carrying.

Hunt

  • Low sanity rolls hunts. A demon is more willing. A shade is less.
  • Hide. Standing still is quieter than walking.
  • Death in the house ends the contract. You respawn with the kit only.

Leave

Survive a correct identify. The house is not the only door in this world — but the contract is what you were sent to finish.
